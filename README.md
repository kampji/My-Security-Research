# My Security Research

My name is Jill Kamperides. I entered cybersecurity in 2019 after being hired at a small consulting firm based in the South Shore of Massachusetts, OCD Tech, having just graduated from the University of Massachusetts Boston with an English degree. I was an entry level analyst assigned solely to a privileged access management program for an international bank, reconciling 1000s of Linux servers daily. After automating a large portion of that process with Python, I was promoted to the penetration testing team.

Since then, I have acquired industry certifications, uncovered critical vulnerabilities (most of which are locked behind clients’ NDAs), registered CVEs, scraped the internet, presented at conferences, and had my work published in hacking magazines. I’ve grown, led, and mentored penetration testing teams, even spanning international borders.

I conduct internal and external penetration tests, mobile and web app penetration tests, wireless tests, AI tests, social engineering, and red teaming.

This repository is for formally tracking my security research. Thanks for reading.
